# global_caption

core_caption:A 10-second stop-motion style 3D clay animation where a small clay figure searches a cozy room for a lost red ball, eventually finding it at his feet, beginning with a close-up of an empty clay plate.
format_and_structure:A continuous narrative short film with dynamic camera tracking and precise framing adjustments, mimicking the tactile, staccato motion of handmade stop-motion animation.
narrative_and_emotional_design:Warm, innocent, and lighthearted. The emotional arc progresses from sudden surprise, to clumsy searching, mild disappointment, and finally joyful relief.
visual_design:The starting state establishes a tactile 3D claymation aesthetic featuring a white-rimmed clay plate on a terracotta surface, illuminated by a warm yellow lamp. The environment features pastel blue walls, a wooden floor, and soft volumetric lighting. The materials display handmade imperfections, such as subtle thumbprints, minor dents, and uneven edges.
audio_design:Cheerful, warm ukulele and xylophone background music, layered with animated Foley including clay footsteps, soft thuds of interacting objects, a rolling ball, and a short, expressive character gasp. No dialogue.

continuity_requirements:Strictly maintain the lighting, color, and texture of the starting plate and terracotta surface throughout the initial camera movement. | The clay figure must maintain consistent proportions: a large round head, small body, black dot eyes, yellow knit vest, blue shorts, and red shoes. | Character movements must exhibit rigid, stiff-jointed articulation typical of stop-motion, completely avoiding fluid CGI morphing or limb stretching. | The physical volume of the red ball and blue button must remain constant when handled.
